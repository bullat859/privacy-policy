Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Dynamic Edge Gesture Control. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and permissions required by the application.

Permissions and Data Usage

To provide its core functionality, Dynamic Edge Gesture Control requires specific permissions. We believe in complete transparency about why these are needed:

Accessibility Service API: This app utilizes the Accessibility Service API to detect touch gestures on the screen edges and execute system-level actions (e.g., navigating Back, going Home, opening Recent Apps, taking Screenshots).

Important: We do NOT use the Accessibility Service to collect, store, or transmit your personal data or screen content. The service is strictly used for detecting predefined gesture triggers and executing your configured navigation actions locally on your device.

Display Over Other Apps (Overlay): This permission allows the app to render the dynamic edge sliders, notch controls, and Pie menus on top of other applications, ensuring they are always accessible when you need them.

Location Permission (Optional): If you use the situational notch triggers linked to Google Maps, the app may request location access to allow you to pick a specific location on the map. We do not track your background location or share your location data.

Data Collection and Third-Party Services

Dynamic Edge Gesture Control is designed to process data locally. However, we integrate with secure third-party services to support the app:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as device identifiers and IP addresses, to provide personalized advertising. For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash Reporting: We may collect anonymous crash logs to help us identify and fix bugs, improving app stability. This data contains no personally identifiable information.

Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Since most processing occurs locally on your device, the risk of data breaches is minimized. Any data handled by third-party services like AdMob is governed by their strict security protocols.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy or data practices, please contact us at our provided support channel.
