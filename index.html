Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Dynamic Edge Gesture Control. This Privacy Policy details exactly how our application works, what permissions it requests, and how those permissions are strictly used to deliver powerful device automation and gesture controls without compromising your privacy.

How It Works & Permissions Required

Our app provides deep system integration to give you unparalleled control over your device. To achieve this, we rely on the following Android permissions, strictly utilized for local functionality:

1. Accessibility Service API

This is the core engine of the app. We use the Accessibility Service to:

  • Draw the gesture overlays (edge sliders, notches, and pie menus) directly on your screen using the accessibility overlay capability (bypassing the need for standard "Display over other apps").
  • Detect your touch interactions on these drawn edge zones.
  • Execute system-level commands requested by your gestures, such as navigating Back, going Home, opening Recent Apps, or triggering Quick Settings.
Privacy Guarantee: We do NOT use the Accessibility Service to monitor your interactions with other apps, read your screen content, or capture any personal data. It is entirely restricted to detecting touches on our specific edge sliders and performing your configured actions.

2. Location (Background, Fine & Coarse)

Used to enable Situational Notch Triggers. If you configure a gesture to activate based on your physical location (via the Google Maps picker), the app needs to monitor your location in the background to seamlessly trigger the action when you arrive at or leave the configured area.

3. Storage (Manage External & Read/Write)

Provides the app access to your files to support features like the Offline Music Service (playing local audio files via gestures) and managing media or backups associated with your custom configurations.

4. Usage Access (Package Usage Stats)

Allows the app to detect which application is currently actively running on your screen. This is necessary for "App-Specific Gesture Profiles," enabling the edge controls to dynamically change behavior depending on what app you are using.

5. Modify System Settings (Write Settings)

Empowers your gestures to directly toggle system parameters like Screen Brightness, Screen Rotation, and Volume without needing to open the Android settings menu.

6. Camera, Microphone & Bluetooth

These permissions are only utilized when you map a gesture to specific hardware actions, such as turning on the Flashlight, launching a quick Voice Recording, or toggling Bluetooth connections.

7. Contacts & Phone

Used strictly for "Quick Dial" or "Direct Contact" gesture actions, allowing you to launch a call or open a specific contact directly from an edge swipe or pie menu.

Third-Party Services & Data Collection

All gesture processing, configuration, and data handling occur locally on your device. We do not maintain servers to collect or sell your personal data.

  • Google AdMob: We utilize Google AdMob to display Rewarded Video Ads, allowing you to unlock premium features. AdMob may collect device identifiers (like Advertising ID) and IP addresses to serve personalized ads. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Please review this page periodically.
