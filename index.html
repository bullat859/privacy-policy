Privacy Policy
Dynamic Edge Gesture Control

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Dynamic Edge Gesture Control. We believe in complete transparency. This Privacy Policy details exactly how our application works, what permissions it requests, and how those permissions are strictly used to deliver powerful device automation without compromising your personal privacy.

How It Works & Permissions Required

Our app provides deep system integration to give you unparalleled control over your device. To achieve this, we rely on the following Android permissions, strictly utilized for local functionality:

🛠️ Accessibility Service API

This is the core engine of the app. We use the Accessibility Service to:

  • Draw the gesture overlays (edge sliders, notches, and pie menus) directly on your screen using the accessibility overlay capability.
  • Detect your touch interactions exclusively on these drawn edge zones.
  • Execute system-level commands requested by your gestures, such as navigating Back, going Home, opening Recent Apps, or triggering Quick Settings.
Privacy Guarantee We do NOT use the Accessibility Service to monitor your interactions with other apps, read your screen content, or capture any personal data. It is restricted entirely to our specific edge UI and system navigation.

📍 Location (Background, Fine & Coarse)

Used strictly to enable Situational Notch Triggers. If you configure a gesture to activate based on your physical location (via the Google Maps picker), the app monitors your location in the background to seamlessly trigger the action when you arrive at or leave the configured area.

📊 Usage Access (Package Usage Stats)

Allows the app to detect which application is currently actively running on your screen. This is necessary for "App-Specific Gesture Profiles," enabling the edge controls to dynamically adapt their behavior depending on what app you are currently using.

⚙️ Modify System Settings (Write Settings)

Empowers your gestures to directly toggle system parameters like Screen Brightness, Screen Rotation, Wi-Fi, and Volume without needing to open the Android settings menu.

💾 Storage (Manage External & Read/Write)

Provides the app access to your files to support local features like the Offline Music Service (playing local audio files via gestures) and managing media or backups associated with your custom configurations.

🎙️ Camera, Microphone & Bluetooth

These permissions are only utilized when you map a gesture to specific hardware actions, such as turning on the Flashlight, launching a quick Voice Recording, or toggling Bluetooth connections.

📞 Contacts & Phone

Used strictly for "Quick Dial" or "Direct Contact" gesture actions, allowing you to launch a call or open a specific contact directly from an edge swipe or pie menu.

Third-Party Services & Data Collection

All gesture processing, configuration, and data handling occur locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers to collect or sell your personal data.

  • Google AdMob: We utilize Google AdMob to display Rewarded Video Ads, allowing you to unlock premium features. AdMob may collect device identifiers (like Advertising ID) and IP addresses to serve personalized ads. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Dynamic Edge Gesture Control processes your preferences locally, all configurations, profiles, and offline data are stored directly on your device. If you wish to delete your data, you simply need to clear the app's cache/data via your Android settings or uninstall the application. Since we do not transmit this data to any external servers, uninstalling the app permanently removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.
